Quick Capture

Save anything you're reading to a friend's profile — without leaving the page.

1 · Pin the icon so it's always one click away

Browsers can't pin it for you, so:

  1. Chrome / Edge / Brave: click the puzzle-piece 🧩 in the toolbar → find Communicator → click the pin 📌.
  2. Firefox: right-click the toolbar → Customize, or click the extensions icon and pin it.

2 · Highlight something and right-click

Select text on any page — a LinkedIn update, an article, a comment — right-click it, and choose "Add note to a friend…". Pick who it's about, confirm, done. No copy-paste, no switching tabs.

You can also right-click a link or an image the same way.

3 · One click after the first time

Once you've picked a friend, the right-click menu remembers them — "Add to [Name]" saves instantly with no popup. Picking someone else always goes through "Add note to a friend…".

4 · Or open the popup directly

Click the ◆ icon any time to write a note from scratch, pick a friend manually, or drop a file (photo, PDF, whatever) onto their profile.
